# Bureau Graphics content replacement checklist

The redesigned site deliberately omits unconfirmed claims. Complete this checklist as real business information and approved assets become available.

## Priority 1: real photography

Keep the current filenames when replacing an image so no code changes are required. Use landscape images at least 1600 × 1000 px and keep the main subject away from the outer 8%.

| Current file | Replace with |
| --- | --- |
| `public/visuals/branding-hero.webp` | Best branded product collection: stationery, merchandise, signage or campaign set |
| `public/visuals/aviation-publications.webp` | QRH, logbook, checklist, lanyard, notebook and aviation merchandise arrangement |
| `public/visuals/aviation-range.webp` | Strong aviation publication range or operator job |
| `public/visuals/print-production.webp` | Actual shop floor, press or production team at work |
| `public/visuals/uv-products.webp` | 3–5 strong UV products on a clean surface |
| `public/visuals/large-format.webp` | 3–5 real signs, banners, window graphics or display jobs |
| `public/visuals/finishing.webp` | 3–5 bound, laminated or assembled documents |

Also photograph the building entrance and the route from Wilson Airport Exit Lane to KAA Building No. 49 for the location section.

## Priority 2: brand and trust assets

- Supply the Bureau Graphics logo as a clean SVG.
- Confirm written permission to display each client logo currently shown.
- Replace raster client logos with SVG versions where available.
- Supply 2–3 real testimonials with the person’s name, organisation, role and permission to publish.
- Supply recent project captions: client or sector, brief, production route and finished result.

## Priority 3: business facts to confirm

- Weekly opening hours and holiday exceptions.
- Normal WhatsApp/email reply time. Only publish a target the team can consistently meet.
- Collection and delivery areas, charges and any courier conditions.
- Payment methods: M-Pesa Till or Paybill, cards, bank transfer and cash as applicable.
- Typical turnaround ranges by service.
- Minimum practical quantities by service.
- Approved “from” prices, only if the team wants public pricing.
- Final production domain.
- Domain email such as `hello@yourdomain`.
- Exact Facebook, Instagram and Google Business Profile URLs.

## Priority 4: future functionality

- Connect a secure file-upload provider only when storage, access, retention and deletion rules have been chosen.
- Add a database or CRM only if quote tracking, job status, customer accounts or an admin dashboard becomes a real requirement.
- Add analytics only after choosing the measurements and publishing the required privacy/cookie notice.

Until then, the quote builder intentionally stores the draft only in the visitor’s browser and opens WhatsApp or email for the actual enquiry and artwork attachment.
